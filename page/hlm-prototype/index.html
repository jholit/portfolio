Leonardo Debruz

Nice. What are the main design decisions I should notice?

7/05/2026 - 10:44 AM

HLM AI

Three areas do the heavy lifting: a simplified menu panel, a centralized prompting control area, and a chat workspace built around familiar messaging patterns.

The menu makes frequent actions easier to recognize, the prompter keeps models and capabilities in one clear hub, and the conversation uses natural language and familiar structure to feel easier to understand and engage with. Cleaner, friendlier, and much easier to scan.
