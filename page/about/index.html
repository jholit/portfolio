Hello, Jack here!

As a Product Designer I believe the best products are ones users don’t remember being complicated to use. My goal in a digital world is to reduce frustration, create clear system processes, and help people reach the end result of the tech products used in everyday life. Did I mention this also means straightforward terminology that we all understand?

Beyond designing, I am a PC video gamer at heart - 18 years and going strong and a world-builder because...honestly...I no idea! What I know though is that both relate to how I think about systems, interactions, and experiences.
