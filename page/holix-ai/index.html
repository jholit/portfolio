HOLIX Ai

HOLIX Ai is a functional product design concept for an organized, approachable AI workspace.

It began as a response to problems Jaqweal Holit experienced while using ChatGPT, including scattered controls, unclear navigation, and an interface that could become difficult to manage as more features were added.

The project developed into its own product direction focused on three goals: making AI controls easier to understand, keeping important tools close to the user’s input, and making conversations clearer to read and revisit.

It also demonstrates Jaqweal’s end-to-end product design process, from identifying usability problems and defining the product structure to designing the interface, representing interaction states, and implementing the working front end.

What are the main product decisions I should pay attention to?

7/05/2026 - 10:44 AM

HOLIX Ai

The experience is built around three connected areas: the menu panel, the prompter control area, and the chat.

The menu panel acts as the workspace’s organizational center. It gives conversations, tools, and important actions a clear structure instead of allowing features to accumulate without hierarchy.

The prompter control area places AI input, model options, capabilities, and supporting tools close together. This reduces unnecessary movement across the interface and allows users to adjust how they interact with the AI while composing a prompt.

The chat uses familiar communication patterns, including clear speaker identities, portraits, names, and timestamps. These details make longer conversations easier to scan, understand, and reference later.

Together, these decisions create an AI workspace that can expand while remaining familiar and organized.

What does the working prototype actually prove?

7/05/2026 - 10:47 AM

HOLIX Ai

It proves that the design exists beyond static presentation screens.

The navigation, menus, prompt tools, and chat controls are interactive. The interface adapts across supported large-screen layouts, and its major hover, focus, selected, open, and active states are represented.

It also demonstrates how the product’s structure supports future growth. New tools and capabilities can be added through the menu and prompter systems without requiring users to relearn the entire workspace.

The product interface and its core interactions are implemented. Only connection to a live AI model is simulated because AI infrastructure and model development are outside the scope of this product design prototype.
